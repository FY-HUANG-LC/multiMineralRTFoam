# multiMineralRTFoam
An OpenFOAM-based solver for multi-mineral dissolution and reactive transport.
Overview
multiMineralRTFoam is a research solver developed with OpenFOAM 7 to study coupled single-phase flow, reactive transport, and mineral dissolution in heterogeneous porous media. Using a micro-continuum framework, it examines how mineral-specific dissolution kinetics and evolving pore structures affect reactive accessibility and fluid flow.

The study considers fast-reacting (F), slow-reacting (S), and inert (I) mineral phases. Dissolution of slow-reacting minerals may open transport pathways and expose previously shielded fast-reacting surfaces, leading to a kinetic unlocking mechanism.
